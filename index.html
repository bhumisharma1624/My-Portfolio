01 / About

A little about me

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science student (GGSIPU, 2026) with a growing focus on user-centered digital design. My work sits at the intersection of design and code — I prototype in Figma, then understand exactly how those screens get built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

I've completed a UI/UX design internship at CodSoft, an Internshala UI/UX certification, and a Deloitte Australia Cyber Job Simulation via Forage. Alongside design, I work with Power BI for data visualization and I'm building my foundations in Machine Learning and AI.
